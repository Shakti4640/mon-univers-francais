Quick Reference

The imparfait describes:

  • Ongoing actions in the past (was doing)
  • Habitual/repeated actions in the past (used to do)
  • Descriptions in the past (it was, there were)

Formation:

  1. Take the nous form of the present tense
  2. Drop the -ons
  3. Add the imparfait endings
SubjectEndingExample: parler (nous parlons → parl-)
je-aisparlais
tu-aisparlais
il/elle/on-aitparlait
nous-ionsparlions
vous-iezparliez
ils/elles-aientparlaient

⚠️ ONLY ONE irregular stem: ÊTRE → ét-
(All other verbs follow the regular formation from the "nous" present tense stem.)

Quick Reference — When to Use the Imparfait

UseExampleTranslation
Habitual/repeated pastJe lisais tous les soirs.I used to read every evening.
Ongoing actionIl pleuvait quand je suis sorti.It was raining when I went out.
Background descriptionLe ciel était bleu.The sky was blue.
Physical/emotional stateElle était triste.She was sad.
AgeJ'avais 12 ans.I was 12 years old.
Time/weatherIl était midi. Il faisait froid.It was noon. It was cold.
Simultaneous actionsPendant qu'il cuisinait, elle lisait.While he was cooking, she was reading.
Polite requests (softened)Je voulais vous demander...I wanted to ask you...

Key time expressions often used with imparfait:
toujours, souvent, d'habitude, normalement, chaque jour/semaine/mois/année, tous les jours, le lundi (on Mondays), autrefois, jadis, à cette époque, quand j'étais petit(e), pendant que

Exercise I — Stylistic Rewriting

  1. Imparfait de rupture: Le 11 novembre 1918, la guerre prenait fin.
    Effect: The imparfait slows down the moment, making the reader linger on this historic event. It creates a cinematic, panoramic feel — as if time itself is suspended at this turning point.

  2. Imparfait contrefactuel: Sans cette rencontre, il n'écrivait jamais ce livre.
    Effect: More colloquial and vivid than the conditionnel passé. Creates a sense of immediacy and inevitability — as if we can almost see the alternative reality unfolding.

  3. Discours indirect libre: Marie regardait par la fenêtre. Son mari ne l'aimait plus. Elle devait partir. Il n'y avait plus rien à faire.
    Effect: We enter Marie's mind directly, without the distance of "elle pensait que..." The reader experiences her thoughts as she does, creating intimacy and emotional immediacy.

  4. Imparfait de rupture: Deux semaines plus tard, le célèbre peintre mourait dans la pauvreté.
    Effect: The precise time marker ("deux semaines plus tard") combined with the imparfait creates a poignant, lingering image of the painter's death. It feels like a slow fade-out rather than an abrupt event.

  5. Imparfait contrefactuel: Un instant de plus et la maison était emportée par la crue.
    Effect: The imparfait makes the near-catastrophe feel more real and immediate than the conditionnel passé. The reader almost feels the house being swept away — it creates a visceral sense of "how close it was."
