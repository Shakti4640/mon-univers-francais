Quick Reference

The futur simple describes actions that will happen in the future. It is used more in written/formal French than the futur proche, and for predictions, promises, and general future events.

English equivalent: will do / will be / will eat, etc.

Formation:
INFINITIVE + FUTURE ENDINGS

For -ER and -IR verbs, add endings directly to the infinitive.
For -RE verbs, drop the final -e before adding endings.

Endings (same for ALL verbs):

SubjectEnding
je-ai
tu-as
il/elle/on-a
nous-ons
vous-ez
ils/elles-ont

⚠️ Notice: These endings look like the present tense of avoir (ai, as, a, ons, ez, ont)!

Regular Examples:

Subjectparler (to speak)finir (to finish)vendre (to sell)
jeparleraifiniraivendrai
tuparlerasfinirasvendras
il/elle/onparlerafiniravendra
nousparleronsfinironsvendrons
vousparlerezfinirezvendrez
ils/ellesparlerontfinirontvendront

Quick Reference — When to Use the Futur Simple

UseExampleEnglish
Future events/predictionsIl pleuvra demain.It will rain tomorrow.
PromisesJe te le promets, je reviendrai.I promise, I will come back.
Formal plansLa réunion commencera à 14h.The meeting will begin at 2pm.
Polite requests (softened)Vous voudrez bien fermer la porte.Would you kindly close the door.
Commands (softened)Tu feras tes devoirs avant de sortir.You will do your homework before going out.
Hypothetical result (si + present)Si tu viens, je serai content.If you come, I will be happy.
Probability/conjectureIl sera malade. (= He must be sick.)He's probably sick.
Historical futureNapoléon mourra en 1821.Napoleon will die in 1821. (narrative)

Key time expressions with futur simple:
demain, après-demain, la semaine prochaine, le mois prochain, l'année prochaine, dans (deux jours/une semaine/un mois), un jour, bientôt, plus tard, quand, lorsque, dès que, aussitôt que

Quick Reference — Futur Simple with Reflexive Verbs

Reflexive verbs in the futur simple work the same as in any other tense — just add the reflexive pronoun before the conjugated verb.

Subjectse lever
jeme lèverai
tute lèveras
il/elle/onse lèvera
nousnous lèverons
vousvous lèverez
ils/ellesse lèveront

Negative: Je ne me lèverai pas tôt.

UseExampleNote
Futur de conjectureCe sera le facteur.That must be the mailman. (present probability)

Exercise D — FP vs. FS

  1. FP: Il va pleuvoir. FS: Il pleuvra. Difference: FP suggests imminent rain (you can see the clouds); FS is a general/neutral prediction.
  2. FP: Je vais partir. FS: Je partirai. Difference: FP sounds more decided/immediate; FS sounds more formal/distant.
  3. FP: Elle va comprendre. FS: Elle comprendra. Difference: FP implies "soon" or "you'll see"; FS is a calmer, more distant prediction.
  4. FP: On va être en retard. FS: Nous serons en retard. Difference: FP is spoken/informal; FS is more formal/neutral.
  5. FP: Ils vont venir. FS: Ils viendront. Difference: FP implies certainty/imminence; FS is a general statement about the future.

Key Takeaways for Futur Simple:

  1. Formation = infinitive (or irregular stem) + -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont
  2. Irregular stems must be memorized (ser-, aur-, ir-, fer-, pourr-, voudr-, devr-, saur-, viendr-, verr-, enverr-, courr-, mourr-, faudr-, pleuvr-, vaudr-)
  3. NEVER futur after "si" = if — Si tu viens (present), je serai (FS) content
  4. ALWAYS futur after quand/lorsque/dès que — Quand tu arriveras (FS), je t'appellerai (FS) — UNLIKE ENGLISH!
  5. Si = whether → futur simple IS allowed — Je ne sais pas s'il viendra
  6. FS for promises — Je te le promets, je reviendrai
  7. FS for probability — Ce sera le facteur = That must be the mailman
  8. FS for politeness — Vous voudrez bien m'excuser
  9. FS for commands — Tu feras tes devoirs = You WILL do your homework
  10. Reported speech — FS → conditionnel after past reporting verb
