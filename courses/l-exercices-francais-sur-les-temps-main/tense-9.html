Quick Reference

The futur antérieur describes an action that will have been completed before another future action or before a specific point in the future.

English equivalent: will have done / will have eaten / will have left, etc.

Formation:
SUBJECT + AUXILIARY (avoir or être) in FUTUR SIMPLE + PAST PARTICIPLE

With AVOIR:

Subjectavoir (futur simple)+ past participle
j'auraiparlé
tuaurasparlé
il/elle/onauraparlé
nousauronsparlé
vousaurezparlé
ils/ellesaurontparlé

With ÊTRE:

Subjectêtre (futur simple)+ past participle
jeseraiallé(e)
tuserasallé(e)
il/elle/onseraallé(e)(s)
nousseronsallé(e)s
vousserezallé(e)(s)
ils/ellesserontallé(e)s

⚠️ Same rules as ALL other compound tenses:

  • Same verbs use ÊTRE (DR MRS VANDERTRAMP + reflexives)
  • Same agreement rules apply
  • Same irregular past participles

Timeline:

FUTUR ANTÉRIEUR ←――――― FUTUR SIMPLE ←――――― PRÉSENT
(will have done)         (will do)              (do/does)

Quick Reference — When to Use the Futur Antérieur

The futur antérieur is used in TWO main ways:

1. Action completed BEFORE another future action (after time conjunctions)

PatternExample
Quand + FA, FSQuand j'aurai fini, je sortirai.
Lorsque + FA, FSLorsqu'il sera arrivé, nous partirons.
Dès que + FA, FSDès que tu auras mangé, nous partirons.
Aussitôt que + FA, FSAussitôt qu'elle aura compris, elle agira.
Après que + FA, FSAprès qu'il aura fini, il se reposera.
Une fois que + FA, FSUne fois qu'ils seront partis, je rangerai.

⚠️ In English, we use the present perfect after "when/as soon as":

  • "When I have finished, I will leave."
  • In French: "Quand j'aurai fini, je partirai." (FUTUR ANTÉRIEUR!)

2. Conjecture / Probability about the past (standalone)

ExampleMeaning
Il n'est pas là. Il sera parti.He's not here. He must have left.
Elle ne répond pas. Elle se sera endormie.She's not answering. She must have fallen asleep.

Quick Reference — Futur Antérieur with Reflexive Verbs

All reflexive verbs use ÊTRE in the futur antérieur (same as all other compound tenses).

SubjectExample: se lever
jeme serai levé(e)
tute seras levé(e)
ilse sera levé
ellese sera levée
onse sera levé(s)
nousnous serons levé(e)s
vousvous serez levé(e)(s)
ilsse seront levés
ellesse seront levées

⚠️ Same agreement rules as all compound tenses with être:

  • Agreement with reflexive pronoun when it's the direct object
  • No agreement when there's a separate direct object

Exercise A — Futur Antérieur in Reported Speech

When the reporting verb is in the present, the FA stays the same.
When the reporting verb is in the past, the FA shifts to conditionnel passé.

Present reporting verb (no shift):

  1. Marie dit : « J'aurai fini avant midi. »
    → Marie dit qu'elle ________ ________ avant midi.

  2. Il affirme : « Nous serons partis avant leur arrivée. »
    → Il affirme qu'ils ________ ________ avant leur arrivée.

Past reporting verb (FA → conditionnel passé):

  1. Marie a dit : « J'aurai fini avant midi. »

🔑 ANSWER KEY — Exercise H

  1. aurai fini — FA — Action completed before another future action (main clause in FS: "sortirai")
  2. avais fini — PQP — Action completed before another past action (main clause in PC: "suis sorti"); spoken register
  3. eus fini — PA — Action completed immediately before another past action (main clause in PS: "sortis"); literary register
  4. ai fini — PC — Simple completed past action with a specific time; no "before another action" relationship
  5. avais fini — PQP — Action completed before another past action ("avant qu'il arrive" = before he arrived)
  6. aurai fini — FA — Action that will be completed by a future point ("d'ici demain")
  7. sera, parti — FA (conjecture) — Probability about a past event; "he must have already left"
  8. aurait fini — Conditionnel passé — FA shifted to cond. passé in reported speech after past reporting verb ("a dit"). Original direct speech would be: "J'aurai fini avant midi."

Key Takeaways for Futur Antérieur:

  1. Formation = avoir/être in FUTUR SIMPLE + past participle
  2. Same auxiliary, agreement, and past participle rules as all other compound tenses
  3. Primary use = action completed BEFORE another future action (after quand, dès que, etc.)
  4. "D'ici" + FA = "by" a certain future time
  5. Conjecture about the past = Il sera parti = He must have left (standalone)
  6. Retrospective assessment = Il aura été le meilleur = He will have been the best
  7. After quand/dès que = FA (not present perfect like English!)
  8. Reported speech = FA → conditionnel passé (after past reporting verb)
  9. Passive FA = aura été + past participle
  10. Completes the four compound tenses = PC (present), PQP (imparfait), PA (passé simple), FA (futur simple)
